# World English Bible — the free Bible app

The complete **World English Bible (WEB)** as a free, installable web app — the whole Bible, word for word as published, public domain.

**Live:** https://jesuslovestyler.github.io/World-English-Bible/
**Home:** https://jesuslovestyler.com/

## What's inside

- **All 66 books**, with **red-letter text across 2,059 verses** (the Gospels, Acts, and Revelation)
- **Chapter helps for all 1,189 chapters** — a big-idea line and section headings for every chapter, written for this app
- **Listen** — a device read-aloud voice, plus David Williams's public-domain recorded reading of the whole Bible, streamed
- **Reading plans**, including an Advent plan, a 30-stop Life of Jesus journey, and the Prayers of the Bible
- **Bible Words glossary** — 404 terms, tap any one while reading
- **Where to Turn** — 100 topics of verses for life's moments
- **64 Doré illustrations** (1866, public domain), saveable for offline
- **The whole Bible as an EPUB** download, red letter included
- **My Journey** — your chapters read, saved verses, notes, and places, with **backup and restore** so your progress is never lost
- Works offline once installed; no account, no ads, nothing to buy

## The text

The World English Bible is a public-domain modern English translation, translated by **Michael Paul Johnson** and many volunteers, published by eBible.org. Johnson is credited in the app and in the EPUB, as he should be. The verse text in this app is never altered; the chapter helps were written for the app and are given away with it.

## The files

`index.html` is the whole app. `assets/` holds the Bible text (`ot.js`, `nt.js`), cross-references (`xrefs.js`), the listening data (`audio.js`), the EPUB, and map images; the Doré plates live there too. Verse data is plain JavaScript — take it and build.
