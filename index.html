      Page counts are estimates, figured from the actual text at this large type size — about 200 words to a page.

      “Save as PDF” makes a real PDF file of the pages you chose — on iPhone the share sheet opens, and “Save to Files” keeps it. “Print” sends the same pages to a printer; in the home-screen app it shows the pages with a Share button instead — tap Share, then Print or Save to Files.

      Get a better voice — free, one time.

      iPhone & iPad: Open Settings → Accessibility → Read & Speak → Voices → English, choose a voice, and download its Enhanced or Premium version. (On older iPhones, Read & Speak is called Spoken Content.) Then pick it in the Voice menu here.

      Android: Open Settings and search 'Text-to-speech', open it, then choose Install voice data → English and download a voice you like. (On Samsung phones it's under General management → Text-to-speech.) Reopen this app and pick it in the Voice menu.

      On a computer, Chrome already includes a natural Google voice.
